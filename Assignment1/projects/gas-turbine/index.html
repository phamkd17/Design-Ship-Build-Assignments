How a Gas Turbine Works

25 versions of a landing page, moving from wide exploration to a final design. Each card links to a version and notes what was tried and learned.

Concept Development — v1 to v7

v01
Plain text, no style
Raw content first — just the words, nothing else. Establishes what needs to be said before any design decisions.
v02
SVG stage diagrams
Added inline SVG illustrations for each of the four stages. Tested whether simple diagrams alone could carry the explanation.
v03
Real engine photos
Replaced SVGs with real Wikimedia Commons photos — Rolls-Royce intake, axial compressor GIF, combustor, and GE J79 turbine. Dark layout introduced.
v04
Schematic + stage highlights
Added a clean engine schematic (text stripped) with colored overlays marking each stage, plus a 4-stage flow diagram as an overview at the top.
v05
Applications section
Added a "Where Gas Turbines Are Used" grid, grouped into Thrust Production (aviation, military, missiles) and Power Production (grid, naval, helicopters, pipelines).
v06
Why it matters + engine type schematics
Added a "Why It Matters" paragraph at top; replaced animation with turbofan and turboshaft schematics including a generator diagram.
v07
Big numbers first
Led with four dramatic stats (1,500°C / 40× / 1 ton/s / 60% efficiency) before any explanation. Tested whether raw numbers hook attention better than narrative.

User Interface Exploration — v8 to v11

v08
Interactive stage explorer
Click any of the four stage tabs to highlight that zone on the engine schematic and load the matching photo and text below. First interactive version.
v09
Editorial / light mode
Tried a completely different visual direction — white background, serif body text, magazine-style pull quote. Contrasts sharply with the dark technical aesthetic of earlier versions.
v10
Scroll-reveal storytelling
Each stage panel fades and slides in as you scroll into view using IntersectionObserver. Fixed progress dots on the left track which stage you're reading.
v11
Sticky nav + scroll spy
Sticky pill nav highlights the active stage as you scroll through tall two-column stage sections. First version with contextual navigation.

End Application Exploration — v12 to v20

v12
Accordion stages
All four stages shown as collapsed cards; click to expand and reveal the photo and full description. Only one open at a time.
v13
Split-screen — Renewable Energy & Grid
Audience: grid operators and renewables engineers. Split-screen sticky schematic with CCGT, H₂ co-firing, and DLN content. Left schematic stays fixed while right panel scrolls through each stage.
v14
Comparison table — Power Generation
Audience: utility engineers and plant managers. Comparison table showing gas turbine vs. piston engine across six attributes. Updated with CCGT, GE 7HA, and utility-grid context.
v15
Color panels — Naval & Maritime
Audience: naval engineers and maritime procurement. Each stage fills 100vh with a tinted panel. Content rewritten for GE LM2500, COGAG configurations, DDG-51, and salt-spray inlet separators.
v16
Type-only — Helicopters & Rotorcraft
Audience: rotorcraft engineers and MRO technicians. No photos — diagrams and typography only. Content rewritten for turboshaft architecture: GE T700, free power turbine, gearbox ratio, VTOL UAVs.
v17
Step-through — Oil & Gas Pipelines
Audience: pipeline engineers and O&G operations. One stage per card with Prev/Next navigation. Content covers pipeline compression, wellhead power, Trans-Alaska, 25,000+ hr MTBO.
v18
Data chart — Emergency & Standby Power
Audience: data center and hospital facility managers. Pressure/temperature SVG chart with stage data cards. Content rewritten for <15-second startup, N+1 redundancy, Tier IV uptime requirements.
v19
Mobile-first — Industrial Manufacturing
Audience: plant engineers reviewing on-site. Mobile-first single column with full-width photos and floating stage badges. Content covers industrial CHP, 80%+ total efficiency, DLE combustors, HRSG.
v20
Best-of synthesis — Cogeneration & CHP
Audience: energy managers and sustainability engineers. Combines sticky nav, big stats, interactive schematic, and engine schematics. Content covers CCGT, HRSG, 80%+ CHP efficiency, university campuses, H₂ co-firing.

Technical Depth Exploration — v21 to v24

v21
Editorial + interactive synthesis
Merges v09's light-mode editorial serif aesthetic with v20's sticky nav, big stats, interactive schematic highlights, applications grid, and engine schematics.
v22
For middle schoolers
Fun analogies, big colorful numbers, "Did you know?" wow facts, and a True/False quiz. Written for ages 11–14 with zero jargon.
v23
For graduate students
Full Brayton cycle analysis, isentropic efficiency equations, stage loading, Zeldovich NOx, CMC blades, and hydrogen combustion challenges. Assumes thermodynamics fluency.
v24
For field engineers
Operations manual aesthetic: EGT limits, EOH tracking, borescope intervals, fault symptom tables, surge warning signs, and maintenance checklists. Built for technicians on the job.

Final Design

v25
Application toggle — split-screen final
Editorial intro + stats, then a split-screen sticky schematic with a 7-application toggle (Turbofan, Military, Renewables, Power Gen, Naval, Helicopters, Oil & Gas). Each selection rewrites all four stage descriptions and stats in place.
