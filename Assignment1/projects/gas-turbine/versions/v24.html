Stage-by-Stage: Operational & Maintenance Focus

INTAKE / INLET
  • FOD — Foreign Object Damage: primary risk during start/stop; inspect intake screens per schedule
  • Inlet filtration: self-cleaning pulse-jet or static barrier filters; monitor ΔP across filter (clean: <250 Pa; replace at >500 Pa)
  • Anti-icing: activate when OAT <5°C and visible moisture; inlet icing raises compressor inlet depression and risks surge
  • Inlet depression monitoring: suction side ΔP vs. ambient; rising trend indicates filter blockage or inlet icing
  • Inspect inlet bellmouth for erosion, corrosion, and liner cracking at each planned outage
  • Never run without inlet protection systems in sandy/dusty environments — abrasion degrades compressor blades rapidly
HPC / LPC — COMPRESSOR
  • Typical stages: 14–17 HPC + 3–5 LPC (aero-derivative) or 15–20 stages (heavy-frame)
  • Stall indicators: rapid EGT rise, unusual combustion noise, N1/N2 speed fluctuation — initiate controlled shutdown per OEM procedure
  • Surge: violent reversal of airflow; can cause blade failure in <1 sec — surge valve response must be <100 ms
  • Online (crank) washing: demineralized water only; perform during load reduction; frequency: weekly in coastal/dusty environments
  • Offline washing with detergent: restore fouling-induced efficiency loss of 1–3%; schedule every 1,000–2,000 EOH
  • Borescope inspection intervals: HPC stages 1–5 every 4,000 EOH; full compressor at 8,000 EOH — check for tip rubs, FOD nicks, erosion
  • Variable stator vane (VSV) schedule: verify actuation accuracy at scheduled intervals; sticking VSVs reduce surge margin
COMBUSTOR / COMBUSTION SYSTEM
  • Combustion instability (humming/rumble): thermoacoustic oscillations; monitor via accelerometer/pressure taps; adjust fuel staging
  • Hot streaks: non-uniform combustor exit temperature; indicates fuel nozzle coking, poor atomisation, or damaged swirler
  • Pattern factor monitoring: EGT thermocouple spread >30°C above average warrants combustor inspection
  • Fuel nozzle inspection/cleaning: every 8,000 EOH or on EGT spread exceedance; coking increases droplet size, raises local TIT
  • Liner cracking: inspect at each hot section inspection (HSI); hairline cracks in dilution holes are common; throughwall cracks require immediate replacement
  • Cross-igniter check at every start; failed igniter causes wet light-off risk — purge per OEM procedure before re-attempt
  • Transition piece (TP) seals: primary cause of compressor discharge air loss; inspect seals at every HSI
HPT / LPT — TURBINE
  • EGT spread: thermocouple-to-thermocouple variance >40°C indicates hot streak from combustor; >70°C warrants shutdown and investigation
  • Blade creep/oxidation: primary HPT life limiter; Larson–Miller parameter tracking per blade serial number; replace at LMP limit
  • Tip clearance: active clearance control (ACC) valve position and casing temperature monitoring; increasing clearance reduces turbine efficiency ≈0.3%/0.1mm
  • TBC spallation: sudden EGT rise without load change; confirms TBC loss — continued operation accelerates substrate oxidation
  • HPT borescope: stages 1 and 2 at every HSI; LPT at major overhaul; look for oxidation, sulphidation, tip rubs, cracks in leading/trailing edges
  • HPT first-stage inspection: typically 24,000 EOH (major overhaul) or 8,000 EOH HSI depending on fuel quality and start frequency
  • Never exceed published EGT limits — even transient exceedances accelerate creep and may void warranty
⚠
Equivalent Operating Hours (EOH): One peaking start = 5–10 equivalent hours. Frequent starts are the primary accelerant of HSI and overhaul intervals. Track EOH, not just clock hours, when scheduling maintenance.

Engine Configuration Reference

Turbofan — Thrust Configuration

Bypass air carries thrust load. BPR = bypass flow / core flow. Higher BPR = lower SFC = lower EGT for equivalent thrust.

Turboshaft — Shaft Power Configuration

All expansion work extracted via power turbine. Output shaft connected to load via reduction gearbox. EGT lower than turbofan at equivalent OPR — more expansion stages.
