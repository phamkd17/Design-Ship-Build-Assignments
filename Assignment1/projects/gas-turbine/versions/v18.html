Emergency & Standby Power

Reliable Power When It Matters Most

Gas turbines provide critical backup power to hospitals, data centers, and military installations — starting in under 15 seconds, sustaining full load for 72+ hours on stored fuel.

Why It Matters

Gas turbines are the gold standard for large-scale backup and standby power. When a hospital loses grid power, or a military forward operating base needs to generate its own electricity, gas turbines provide the combination of fast start (<15 seconds to rated load), extended run capability (72+ hours on stored fuel), and output density that diesel generators cannot match at scale. Modern standby turbines — like the Solar Turbines Saturn 20 — produce 1–15 MW in packages small enough to transport by truck. In critical facilities, N+1 redundancy (one turbine in reserve) guarantees that any single failure does not cause downtime. As microgrids become standard in hospitals and campuses, gas turbines increasingly serve as the backbone of islanded power systems.
