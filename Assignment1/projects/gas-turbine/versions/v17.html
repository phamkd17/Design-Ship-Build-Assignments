How a Gas Turbine Works

Why It Matters

Gas turbines power your flights, your city's electricity, and the ships that guard the seas. Their extraordinary power-to-weight ratio makes them irreplaceable where size matters. They reach full power in minutes — making them the backbone of grid demand response — and are now evolving toward hydrogen combustion as a bridge to a lower-carbon future.

Where Gas Turbines Are Used

✈️
Commercial Aviation
Powers nearly every jet aircraft worldwide.
🪖
Military Aircraft
Afterburning turbofans for supersonic flight.
⚡
Power Generation
~20% of global electricity, instant startup.
🚁
Helicopters
Turboshafts drive rotor systems efficiently.

Two Types of Gas Turbine

Thrust Engine

Turbofan — bypass air produces efficient thrust.

Power Engine

Turboshaft — shaft power drives generators and rotors.
